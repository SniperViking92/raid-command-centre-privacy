Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information Raid Command Centre uses, why it is needed, how it is stored and how you can control or delete it.

Effective date: 12 November 2026  ·  Last updated: 12 November 2026

Privacy at a glance

Raid Command Centre saves your game-tracking progress and uses an account to synchronise that progress between devices. Apple handles iPhone subscription payments; RevenueCat helps verify subscription access. We do not use your information to serve targeted adverts or sell your personal information.

1. Who operates Raid Command Centre

Raid Command Centre is an independently developed game progression tracker operated by Peter Banks, based in the United Kingdom.

For privacy enquiries, email piotrekbanks@gmail.com.

Raid Command Centre is an unofficial companion application and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Battlestate Games.

2. Information we collect and process

  • Account details: Your email address, a unique account identifier and authentication-related information when you register or sign in. Your password is submitted to our authentication provider, Supabase; we do not need to view your plain-text password.
  • Game progress: Information you enter or mark as complete, such as PMC details, quests, trader progress, Hideout upgrades, bosses, achievements, prestige and settings. This may include a player-chosen character name.
  • Subscription details: Subscription and transaction identifiers, purchase history, entitlement status and related information needed to confirm paid access. Apple handles payment processing; we do not receive your full payment card details.
  • Technical information: Our providers may process device type, operating-system information, IP address, connection details and service logs to operate, secure and troubleshoot the app.
  • Support correspondence: If you email us, we receive the information you include so we can respond.

3. How and why we use information

We use this information to create and protect your account, save and synchronise game progression, provide the features you request, check subscription access, respond to support requests and maintain the security and reliability of the service.

Under UK data protection law, the main legal bases are performance of a contract (providing the account, tracker and subscription features), legitimate interests (security, troubleshooting and support), and legal obligations where records must be retained by law. We do not use personal information for targeted advertising.

4. Services that help run the app

  • Supabase: Provides email authentication, account management and private cloud storage for your progress and previous saved revisions. Supabase privacy information.
  • RevenueCat: Connects subscription status to your app account and processes purchase-related identifiers and technical information. RevenueCat privacy policy.
  • Apple: Processes App Store subscriptions, payments, renewals, refunds and subscription management under its own policies. Apple privacy policy.
  • Game reference sources: The app may retrieve publicly available game information from third-party services such as tarkov.dev. Requests to these services may involve ordinary network information, such as an IP address.
  • GitHub Pages: Hosts this public privacy-policy webpage and may process standard website request information. GitHub privacy statement.

Service providers may process information in countries outside the UK. Where applicable, transfers are subject to relevant legal safeguards and the providers' data protection arrangements.

5. Local storage, cloud saves and retention

Game progress is saved on your device so the tracker can preserve your choices. When you use cloud synchronisation, a copy is stored in Supabase under your signed-in account, along with a limited history of previous revisions for recovery (currently up to 10 earlier cloud revisions).

Account and cloud-progress records are generally kept while the account is active, or until you delete it. Some operational logs, backups and purchase-related records may remain for limited periods under provider retention arrangements or where legally required. Exported backups saved by you are under your control.

We use reasonable measures to protect data, including encrypted network connections and account-based access controls. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

6. Subscriptions and payment information

Paid access to Raid Command Centre on iPhone is managed through Apple's App Store. RevenueCat helps confirm whether an active subscription or introductory offer gives your account access to Premium features.

Deleting your cloud account does not cancel an Apple subscription. To stop future renewals, manage or cancel your subscription separately through Apple Subscriptions or your iPhone's Settings. Apple may retain transaction records independently for payment, refund and legal purposes.

7. Access, deletion and local copies

You can delete your account from within the app by opening Settings → Cloud sync → Delete cloud account and cloud saves and following the confirmation steps. This is designed to delete your Supabase account and associated cloud progress and revision history.

Deleting your cloud account does not automatically remove local progress on your devices, exported backup files or records held independently by Apple and other providers for legitimate or legal reasons. You can remove local copies and exports from your devices separately.

If the in-app deletion option is unavailable or you need help with personal information held by the service, contact piotrekbanks@gmail.com.

8. Your privacy rights

Depending on the circumstances and applicable law, you can ask to access, correct, erase, restrict or receive a copy of your personal information, and object to certain processing. Contact us using the email below to exercise these rights. We may need to verify that you are the account holder before disclosing or changing personal information.

If you are in the United Kingdom and are unhappy with how your information is handled, you can also contact the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).

9. Children

The app is intended for users who are old enough to use the service under applicable age and platform rules. If you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, please contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if the app's features, service providers or data-handling practices change. The date at the top will identify the latest version. Material changes will be communicated where required by law.

Common questions

My progress isn't syncing

Check your internet connection and confirm you're signed into the same Raid Command Centre account on each device. Check the cloud-sync status in Settings. Avoid resetting or deleting data while troubleshooting.

I've already subscribed

In the app, open Settings and use Restore Purchases. Make sure you're using the Apple Account that made the purchase and the correct Raid Command Centre account. Contact support if Premium access still doesn't appear.

Cancel a subscription

Apple manages iPhone subscriptions. Open Apple's subscription management to review or cancel. Deleting your Raid Command Centre account does not automatically cancel your subscription.

Delete my account and cloud saves

Open Settings in the app and select Delete Cloud Account and Cloud Saves. Follow the confirmation prompts. If you cannot access the app or need help with deletion, contact the developer at the email above.

Free trials and billing

Eligible new customers may see a seven-day free trial. The applicable offer, local price and renewal terms are shown in Apple's purchase sheet before you confirm. Subscriptions renew unless cancelled through Apple.

Refunds

Purchases are handled by Apple. For an eligible refund request, use reportaproblem.apple.com. For troubleshooting or app feedback, email the developer.

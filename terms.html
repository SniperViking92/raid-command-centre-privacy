1. About Raid Command Centre

Raid Command Centre is an independent companion app for recording and tracking progression in Escape from Tarkov. It is operated by Peter Banks in the United Kingdom.

It provides manually maintained features such as quest progress, PMC and trader levels, Hideout upgrades, bosses, achievements, prestige and cloud synchronisation. It does not automatically control or modify your game account.

Raid Command Centre is an unofficial application. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Battlestate Games.

You can cancel your Apple subscription at any time using Apple's subscription management, or from your iPhone's Settings. To avoid a renewal charge, cancel before the next billing date; Apple recommends doing so at least 24 hours before the trial or billing period ends.

Deleting your Raid Command Centre account does not cancel your Apple subscription. Cancel your subscription separately if you do not want further renewals.
